Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30 October 2026

Fortune Loan Services ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit our website, submit an inquiry or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your inquiry and the services requested, we may collect:

  • Name, mobile number and email address.
  • City, address and contact information.
  • Employment or business details.
  • Loan type and requested loan amount.
  • Income, existing loan and other financial details voluntarily provided.
  • Information submitted through website forms or communication channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to loan inquiries and service requests.
  • Understand your financial and loan requirements.
  • Contact you regarding inquiries and follow-ups.
  • Coordinate with relevant banks, NBFCs and lending partners where authorised.
  • Improve our customer support and services.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

3. WhatsApp and AI-Assisted Communication

We may use WhatsApp and automated or AI-assisted tools to communicate with customers and respond to inquiries. Information processed through these services may be handled by the relevant technology providers, including Meta and Google, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Please do not share OTPs, passwords, PINs, internet banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive identity information in chatbot conversations.

4. Sharing of Information

Where necessary and authorised, relevant information may be shared with banks, NBFCs, lending partners, technology providers or other service providers for the purposes described in this policy. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information provided to us. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and to meet applicable legal, regulatory and record-keeping requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate record-retention needs. You may also request that we stop contacting you about an inquiry.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
