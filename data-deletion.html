Data Deletion Instructions

Fortune Loan Services – Your Trusted Financial Partner

Fortune Loan Services respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This page explains how you can request deletion of personal data shared with us through our website, WhatsApp communication or other services.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

If you want us to delete your personal information, please send a request to our official email address.

To request deletion, email:
legal@fortuneloan.services

Email subject: Personal Data Deletion Request

2. Information to Include

Please provide the following information so we can identify your request:

  • Your full name.
  • Your registered email address, if applicable.
  • Your WhatsApp or contact number used to communicate with us.
  • A brief description of the data you want deleted.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, bank account credentials, or unnecessary sensitive financial documents in your request.

3. What Happens After Your Request?

  1. We will acknowledge receipt of your request.
  2. We may contact you to verify your identity.
  3. We will review the information and identify data eligible for deletion.
  4. We will delete or anonymise eligible information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  5. We will inform you about the outcome of your request.

4. Information We May Need to Retain

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable laws, regulatory requirements, legitimate business records or to resolve disputes and protect legal rights. Where retention is necessary, we will handle that information in accordance with applicable requirements.

5. Third-Party Services

If your information has been shared with a bank, NBFC, WhatsApp, or another service provider as part of your inquiry or application, deletion from our systems may not automatically delete information held independently by that third party. You may need to contact the relevant organisation directly.

7. Updates to This Page

We may update these instructions when our privacy practices or applicable requirements change. Please revisit this page for the latest information.
