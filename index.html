SPECIALIZED SUPPORT

Solutions for complex profiles

Structured, policy-based assistance for genuine financial requirements. Final approval remains with the respective lender.

CIBIL

Genuine Solutions for Poor CIBIL Profiles

We may consider low CIBIL scores, EMI bounces, settled/closed accounts and past credit issues where there is a genuine reason and current repayment capacity.

  • Low / poor CIBIL score
  • EMI bounces or late payments
  • Settled or closed loan accounts
  • Business or medical setbacks that are now resolved
EMI

EMI Restructuring & Stress Reduction

Guidance on lender-approved restructuring and tenure modification for clients facing high EMI pressure, subject to lender policy and approval.

  • Multiple EMI burden
  • Reduced income
  • Temporary financial setbacks
  • Policy-based restructuring options
LAP

Property-Based Loan Support

Property valuation is considered on current market value using independent/authorised valuation processes. Future appreciation is not used for initial sanction.

  • Loan Against Property guidance
  • Current-value based assessment
  • LTV-based structuring
  • Top-up and balance-transfer guidance
Important: We do not support fake documents, inflated valuations, intentional defaults or guaranteed approvals. Loan sanctions, rates, terms and disbursement are decided by the respective Bank/NBFC.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Fortune Loan Services provide loans directly?

No. Fortune Loan Services acts as a financial advisor and facilitator. Final approval and disbursement are handled by the respective Bank or NBFC.

Are services available across India?

Yes. The live website states Pan-India coverage across all States and Union Territories, subject to product and lender availability.

How is eligibility assessed?

Profiles may be assessed using CIBIL/credit history, income, employment or business profile, liabilities, documents and lender-specific criteria.

What documents are commonly required?

PAN, Aadhaar/address proof, income proof such as salary slips or ITR, bank statements and employment/business details are commonly requested, depending on the case.

Are interest rates decided by Fortune Loan Services?

No. Interest rates, tenure and final loan terms are determined by the respective lender under its internal credit policy.

How are documents shared with lenders?

The live site states that documents are internally analysed first and shared only with selected lenders after analysis and with the client's consent.
